# DragonPetApp - Client

This is the client-side of the DragonPetApp, a React Native application designed to track and display the progress of your dragon as you complete various physical activities such as walking, running, and more.

## Features

- **Track Miles, Calories, and Steps**: Log your physical activities and see the progress of your dragon.
- **Strava Integration**: Connect your Strava account to sync your activities.
- **Custom Animations**: Enjoy interactive animations as your dragon evolves.
- **Settings Panel**: Easily manage your app settings.
